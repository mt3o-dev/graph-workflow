I've been at my current company for about four years, and I've genuinely enjoyed it, I joined as a mid-level engineer on the checkout team and grew into owning our payment-retry and fraud-detection architecture end to end. That's exactly why I'm looking now rather than staying complacent: I've solved most of the hard, novel problems this particular platform has to offer at my level, and the growth path here would mean waiting a long time for a staff opening that isn't guaranteed to open soon. I'm not leaving because of any conflict or dissatisfaction, my manager knows I'm exploring and has been supportive, it's simply that I want to work on a system with different scale characteristics and harder distributed-systems problems than the ones I've already solved here. I'm specifically drawn to this role because it involves payments at a scale and complexity that would stretch me in new ways, particularly around multi-currency settlement, which is something I've only touched at the edges. I want my next few years to build genuinely new skills, not repeat variations of what I already know well.
